Information the app handles

The app works with what you enter, and keeps it on your device.

InformationWhere it is keptSent to us?

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts, does not use HealthKit and does not require an account. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Predictions (next period, fertile window, ovulation) are calculated on your device from the days you log, and are not sent to us.

How the information is used

Your data is used inside the app to show your calendar and predictions. The app does not send your data over the internet. It only connects through Apple’s services in two cases that you start yourself:

  • TipsIf you choose to view tips, the app asks Apple’s App Store for current prices, and any tip is processed by Apple through In-App Purchase under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We receive standard sales reports from Apple, but no cycle data. Tips unlock nothing.
  • Feature requestsIf you send a feature request, the app opens your own mail app with a pre-filled email. Nothing is sent unless you send it. The email contains what you type and a short line saying it came from the app; no cycle data is added. Your mail app may include its own details, such as your email address and signature.

Weekly reminder. The reminder is off by default. If you turn it on, the app schedules one silent notification on your device every Sunday afternoon. It is delivered by iOS, not through any server. Its text is the same every week and doesn’t include your dates, phases or other cycle details, and its timing doesn’t depend on your cycle.

Storage, security and retention

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. We don’t have a copy and can’t access it. We’ve designed the app to protect your data, but no method of storage is completely secure.

  • iOS Data ProtectionThe app uses iOS Complete Data Protection, so its database is encrypted by the system while your iPhone is locked.
  • Not backed upThe app excludes its database from iCloud and computer backups, so it isn’t copied to Apple’s servers or your computer. This also means your data can’t be restored from a backup and may not move to a new iPhone. If you lose, reset or replace your device, your data will most likely be lost.
  • Optional passcode encryptionIn Settings → Data encryption you can encrypt the database with a passcode you choose (SQLCipher). The app doesn’t save the passcode, so if you forget it, your data can’t be recovered. Encryption is only as strong as your passcode: longer passcodes that include letters are much harder to guess. You can also set a wipe passcode that erases your data when entered.
  • DeletionThe app is set to overwrite deleted entries in its database rather than just marking them as deleted. Because of how storage works, we can’t guarantee every trace is physically erased straight away, but any remaining data stays protected by iOS encryption.

Because we keep no copy of your cycle data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Feature-request emails are kept only as long as we need them to handle your request.

Sharing and disclosure

We don’t sell, rent or share your cycle data, and we don’t receive it.

  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your cycle data isn’t stored on our servers, so we have none to provide to others, including in response to legal requests. We may disclose information we do hold, such as feature-request emails, where the law requires it.

Information from Apple. Like any App Store app, Apple provides us with aggregated statistics (such as download counts) and, if you’ve chosen to share them in iOS Settings, crash reports. These contain technical details about the app, not the dates you log. Apple also knows that your Apple Account downloaded the app and any tips you bought.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to read, consider and reply to your request.

Your rights and choices

You control the data on your device.

  • Delete everythingSettings → Delete all data permanently erases every logged day from the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.
  • EditSelect any day on the calendar to log or remove it.
  • ReminderTurn the weekly reminder on or off in Settings, or turn off notifications for the app in iOS Settings.
  • EncryptionTurn passcode encryption on or off in Settings at any time.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the GDPR (EU/UK) and US state laws such as the CCPA may give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information. Your cycle data is only on your device, so you can view, change and delete it directly in the app. For anything you’ve sent us by email, contact us and we’ll respond in line with the law that applies to you. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator (in Australia, the OAIC).

Children

The app itself does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Not medical advice

Predictions are estimates based on the days you log and may not be accurate. MYperiod is not a medical device or a contraceptive, and must not be relied on to prevent pregnancy. Talk to a doctor about any health concerns.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date above and publish the new version at royalsoftworks.com.au/myperiod/privacy-policy. If a future version of the app would send your data off your device, we will update this policy before that version is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
