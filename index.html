Simple by design

  • Private by default

    Your data stays on your iPhone. It isn’t sent to us, and it’s left out of iCloud and computer backups.

  • See what’s ahead

    Predicted periods, fertile window, ovulation and each cycle phase, worked out on your device from the days you log.

  • Optional passcode

    Encrypt your data with a passcode of your own, so it can’t be read even when your phone is unlocked.

  • Your whole year

    Pinch out to see twelve months at a glance, with past and predicted periods marked.

  • 20 languages

    Follows your iPhone’s language, with support for VoiceOver, larger text and right-to-left layouts.

  • Free, always

    Every feature is free. There are no ads or subscriptions, just an optional tip if you’d like to say thanks.
